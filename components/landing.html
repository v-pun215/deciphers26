Instructions

The platform encompasses an Online Math Hunt in which students can make their way through math levels created by us.

At each level, the students will encounter a problem which shall point to one answer. Each level has one correct answer.

Answers will always be lower-case, alphanumeric and will contain no spaces. Special characters are allowed. For example, if the answer is 99, you would type it in as 99.

All levels have to be done in a sequential order. No levels can be skipped and the order of levels cannot be changed by own will.

You can come back to this page any time by clicking on the logo in the top left corner of the page.
